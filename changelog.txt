Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-06-06
  Features:
    - Mod settings to tune the fleet. Per-tier startup settings for gun count
      (1-4) and a resistance multiplier on each hunter. A runtime setting to
      toggle friendly fire. Adjust in Settings > Mod settings.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.3.1
Date: 2026-06-06
  Changes:
    - The Vehicle Hangar now requires electricity (1MW) instead of being
      free-powered, and emits pollution while running.
    - Rate-limited the explosive hunters to reduce wasted overkill: Heavy now
      has 2 rocket launchers (was 4) and Nuclear has 1 (was 4), so they fire a
      smaller volley per cooldown instead of dumping a barrage at one spot.
    - Hunters now deploy with a full stack of ammo per gun slot.
    - Disabled friendly fire on the player force so hunters no longer kill
      themselves or each other with their own weapons (e.g. Cannon/Siege shells).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-06-06
  Features:
    - The Vehicle Hangar is now an assembling-machine with selectable hunter
      recipes. Vehicle ingredient sets the chassis, ammo ingredient sets the
      weapon. Three tiers: Light (car + yellow magazines), Medium (tank + red
      magazines), Heavy (tank + explosive rockets). Crafted hunters auto-deploy.
    - Hunters are tinted by tier (Light green, Medium amber, Heavy red).
    - Hunters now keep hunting: after clearing an area they retarget the next
      nearest enemy anywhere on the surface instead of idling.
    - Lowered hunter health (Light 150, Medium 500, Heavy 1200) so they can
      actually be lost and need replacing.
    - Fleet expansion: Cannon Hunter (tank + explosive cannon shells), Siege
      Hunter (spidertron + uranium cannon shells), and Nuclear Hunter
      (spidertron + atomic bombs). Nuclear Hunters are immune to nuclear blasts
      (their own and others'); the blast still damages other friendly units and
      buildings, so deploy them away from your base.
